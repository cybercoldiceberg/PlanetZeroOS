## Development Workflow & AI Transparency
PlanetZero OS is **not vibe-coded**. 

* **Manual Engineering:** Every line of Ada, Assembly, and Forth is manually written, analyzed, and understood at the hardware and mathematical level—never blindly copy-pasted.
* **AI as a Tool, Not a Crutch:** I use AI assistants strictly as a technical reference and debugging aid while building and testing the system myself. All architectural decisions, privilege ring logic, and low-level debugging are handled independently.
