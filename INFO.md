# Welcome to PlanetZero OS

Welcome to PlanetZero, broskies!

## Project Philosophy & Independence
PlanetZero OS is a solo, bare-metal operating system built from scratch in **Ada**, **Assembly**, and **Forth**. 

**A quick heads-up on contributions:**
* **Solo Development:** I do not want outside help, pull requests, or code contributions on this project. I am an independent developer who enjoys the challenge of building and debugging the entire architecture myself.
* **Show Support:** Feel free to star the repo, follow the progress, test builds in QEMU, and share feedback—hopefully this goes big!
* **License:** Everything here is strictly locked under **GPLv2**. You are free to study or fork the code under GPLv2 terms, as long as your derivatives stay open-source.
